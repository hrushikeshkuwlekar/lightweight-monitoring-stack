🧱 App capacity

Like a web server's thread pool, the app processes at most worker slots requests at once. Extra requests wait in a queue — so when the app is overloaded, response time grows while TPS stays flat.

Max TPS ≈ worker slots ÷ time per request. With 4 slots and 0.35 s requests: 4 ÷ 0.35 ≈ 11 TPS.
